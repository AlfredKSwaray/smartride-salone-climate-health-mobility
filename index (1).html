PROTOTYPE · v0.1 · LIVE IN FREETOWN
When roads flood, children die waiting.
SMARTRIDE SALONE changes that.
REAL SCENARIO · EAST FREETOWN
It is 2 AM. Heavy rainfall has flooded Kissy Road — the only route from a child's home to Ola During Children's Hospital, Sierra Leone's only university children's hospital. The caregiver has no way of knowing the road is blocked until they are already stuck in floodwater. Minutes pass. The child's condition worsens.
SMARTRIDE SALONE exists to prevent that moment.
One Problem. One Focus.
This platform does one thing with precision: emergency routing to healthcare facilities during floods. When a road is blocked, the system flags it the moment a community member reports it, displays safe alternative routes instantly, and alerts anyone heading that direction. For the first time, a caregiver or health worker in Sierra Leone can make a safe, informed decision — before they move.
⚡ THE GAME-CHANGING MOMENT
A community member reports a blocked road at 1:47 AM.
By 1:48 AM, every caregiver, driver, and health worker planning that route sees a live disruption alert on the map. An alternative route is already highlighted. A child who might have been stranded in floodwater is now on a safe road, heading to hospital.
"That is not a feature. That is a child reaching the hospital in time."
